Cryptographic Safety

Protected by SQLCipher, AES-256-GCM, and Ed25519 tokens. Data is encrypted before leaving your app, with emergency auto-nuke for rogue devices.

Peer-to-Peer Sync

The NexVault Grid discovers trusted devices over an authenticated signaling relay, then upgrades to encrypted WebRTC channels. No cloud storage.

Tamper Monitor

A cryptographic manifest watches your local database files. If the vault is modified outside the app, integrity checks catch it before damage spreads.

Security Model

> bootstrap

Each app generates Ed25519 identity keys. A signed bootstrap token pairs devices — nothing touches a server.

> offline_lockdown

A 24-hour security heartbeat runs on every device. Lose connection past the deadline and the vault locks down; stay dark and the auto-nuke wipes keys.

> encryption

Vault payloads are AES-256-GCM encrypted locally with SQLCipher and PBKDF2-derived master keys. The relay only sees routing metadata.

> autofill

The browser extension talks to your local desktop vault over a local socket and autofills credentials — your passwords never transit the internet.
